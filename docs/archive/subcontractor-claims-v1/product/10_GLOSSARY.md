# Construction Commercial Glossary

Definitions are product terminology, not legal advice.

- **BOQ — Bill of Quantities:** Structured list of work items, quantities, units, and rates.
- **Schedule of Values:** Allocation of contract value across activities or work items.
- **Valuation Period:** Reporting period for measuring completed work.
- **Payment Application / Interim Payment Application:** Contractor submission requesting payment for measured work and eligible additions.
- **Payment Certificate / Interim Payment Certificate:** Amount certified by the client, consultant, or main contractor.
- **Variation Order:** Change to scope, quantity, rate, or conditions that may affect value or time.
- **Potential Variation:** Possible change not yet fully instructed or agreed.
- **Claim:** Request for contractual relief, payment, or time supported by records.
- **Retention:** Amount withheld from payments until specified release conditions.
- **Advance Payment:** Upfront payment recoverable from later valuations.
- **Advance Recovery:** Deduction that repays the advance.
- **Materials on Site:** Eligible materials delivered but not yet incorporated into the works.
- **Certified to Date:** Cumulative value officially certified.
- **Claimed to Date:** Cumulative value requested by the subcontractor.
- **Final Account:** Agreed final commercial value after completion and adjustment.
- **Aging:** Classification of receivables by days past due.
- **Contra Charge:** Deduction claimed for cost incurred on behalf of or caused by the subcontractor.
- **Back Charge:** Similar deduction for corrective or replacement cost.
- **Provisional Sum:** Allowance for work not fully defined at contract award.
- **Daywork:** Work valued based on labor, plant, and material records.
- **EOT — Extension of Time:** Approved extension to contractual completion date.
