# Product Vision

## Working name

**Subcontractor Billing & Claims OS for MENA**

The working name is descriptive. A commercial brand should be selected after customer interviews.

## Vision

Become the operating system for commercial administration and cash collection for subcontractors across the Middle East and North Africa.

## Mission

Reduce the time, uncertainty, and revenue leakage between completing work on site and receiving cash.

## Strategic problem

Subcontractors commonly manage commercial workflows across disconnected spreadsheets, PDFs, messaging applications, email threads, and physical signatures. This creates:

- Incorrect or incomplete payment applications
- Lost or weakly documented variation claims
- Poor visibility into certification and collection status
- Forgotten retention amounts
- Unclear advance-payment recovery
- Late escalation of cash-flow risk
- Reliance on a small number of experienced quantity surveyors
- Weak audit trails during disputes

## Value proposition

The platform connects contract value, BOQ progress, variations, certificates, invoices, payments, and evidence into one project-level commercial record.

## Positioning

This product is **not** a full ERP and **not** a project scheduling tool. It is a specialized commercial operations platform for subcontractors.

### Category

Construction commercial management SaaS for subcontractors.

### Primary differentiation

- MENA-oriented payment application workflows
- BOQ and valuation logic
- Retention and advance recovery
- Variation and claim evidence management
- Arabic/English bilingual operation
- Cash collection and forecast visibility
- Practical workflows for consultant and main-contractor review

## Product principles

1. **Cash first:** Every feature should improve collection speed, certainty, or evidence.
2. **Contract-driven:** Calculations must be based on recorded contract terms.
3. **Traceable:** Every amount must be explainable back to quantities, rates, approvals, and evidence.
4. **Bilingual by design:** Arabic and English are first-class interfaces.
5. **Operationally simple:** A quantity surveyor should understand the workflow without training in accounting software.
6. **Human-controlled automation:** The platform recommends and calculates; authorized users approve.
7. **No silent mutation:** Financial records cannot be overwritten without versioning and audit history.

## North-star metric

**Certified and collected value managed through the platform per active customer.**

## Supporting metrics

- Time to prepare a payment application
- First-pass acceptance rate
- Days from submission to certification
- Days from certification to payment
- Value of tracked variations
- Value of recovered retention
- Forecast accuracy
- Weekly active commercial users
