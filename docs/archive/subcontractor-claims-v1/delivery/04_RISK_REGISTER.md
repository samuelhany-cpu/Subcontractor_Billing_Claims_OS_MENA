# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| R-01 | Product becomes a generic ERP | High | High | Enforce scope and change control |
| R-02 | Contract rules vary too widely | High | High | Configurable rule engine plus supported patterns |
| R-03 | Customers resist data migration | High | Medium | Import templates and assisted onboarding |
| R-04 | Users continue using spreadsheets | High | High | Fast grid UX, exports, and clear cash-value benefit |
| R-05 | Incorrect calculation damages trust | Medium | Critical | Decimal math, visible breakdowns, test fixtures, review |
| R-06 | Cross-tenant data exposure | Low/Medium | Critical | Defense-in-depth tenancy and security tests |
| R-07 | Arabic layout quality is poor | Medium | High | Native RTL design reviews and pilot testing |
| R-08 | External reviewers refuse new portal | High | Medium | PDF/XLSX and secure-link alternatives |
| R-09 | Sales cycle is longer than expected | Medium | High | Target subcontractors with owner-led decisions |
| R-10 | Custom template work destroys margins | High | High | Template configuration limits and paid onboarding |
| R-11 | Product interpreted as legal advice | Medium | High | Clear disclaimers and source-record traceability |
| R-12 | File storage costs grow unexpectedly | Medium | Medium | Quotas, lifecycle rules, and plan limits |
| R-13 | Forecast perceived as guaranteed | Medium | High | Assumption display and confidence labels |
| R-14 | Large BOQs degrade performance | Medium | High | Server-side grid, indexing, async imports, load tests |
