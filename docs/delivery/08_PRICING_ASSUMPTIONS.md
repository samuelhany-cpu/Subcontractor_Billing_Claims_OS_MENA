# Pricing Assumptions

Pricing must be validated; these are initial hypotheses.

## Suggested value metric

Primary metric: active projects, with user and storage limits.

Why:

- Directly related to customer value
- Easier to understand than per-seat pricing
- Avoids penalizing collaboration
- Scales with commercial workload

## Hypothesis

- Starter: up to 5 active projects
- Growth: up to 20 active projects
- Contractor: up to 50 active projects
- Enterprise: custom

Potential add-ons:

- Guided data migration
- Custom report templates
- Extended storage
- External reviewer portal
- Accounting integration
- Regional hosting
- Premium support

## Pricing validation questions

- What is the cost of one late or rejected application?
- How many employee-hours are spent per monthly application?
- How much retention is currently untracked?
- How many variations are open without complete evidence?
- What software budget does the customer already have?
- Is pricing approval owner-led or procurement-led?
