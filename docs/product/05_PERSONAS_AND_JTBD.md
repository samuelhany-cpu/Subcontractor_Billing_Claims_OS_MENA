# Personas and Jobs to Be Done

## Persona 1 — Commercial Manager

### Context

Responsible for contract value, variations, payment applications, certification, and commercial risk across projects.

### Jobs

- Know what has been claimed, certified, and collected
- Ensure variations are documented and valued
- Identify revenue at risk
- Escalate delayed certifications and payments
- Standardize the work of project quantity surveyors

### Pain points

- Multiple uncontrolled spreadsheets
- Different templates by project
- Missing evidence
- Late discovery of unapproved work
- Difficulty consolidating company-wide cash expectations

## Persona 2 — Quantity Surveyor

### Jobs

- Measure monthly progress
- Prepare payment applications
- Reconcile previous and current quantities
- Price and submit variations
- Respond to reviewer comments

### Pain points

- Repetitive copying across files
- Formula errors
- Slow revision cycles
- Evidence stored across WhatsApp and email
- Unclear certified adjustments

## Persona 3 — Finance Manager

### Jobs

- Issue invoices after certification
- Track due dates
- Allocate payments
- Forecast cash receipts
- Monitor retention and overdue balances

### Pain points

- Certificate information arrives late
- Project teams and finance use different numbers
- Retention schedules are poorly tracked
- Partial payments are difficult to reconcile

## Persona 4 — Project Manager

### Jobs

- Confirm site progress
- Support variation evidence
- Understand commercial effect of delays or instructions
- Help resolve reviewer comments

### Pain points

- Commercial issues are raised after work is completed
- Site evidence is not linked to money
- No clear list of pending approvals

## Persona 5 — Company Owner

### Jobs

- Know expected cash inflow
- Identify risky projects and clients
- Know total receivables and retained money
- Make hiring and financing decisions

### Pain points

- Reports are delayed and inconsistent
- Revenue can look healthy while cash is weak
- High dependence on individual employees

## High-priority JTBD statements

- When month-end arrives, I want to prepare an accurate payment application from approved project data so that I can submit on time.
- When a site instruction changes the work, I want to capture evidence and value immediately so that the variation is not lost.
- When a certificate is issued, I want to compare it with the claim so that I can understand every deduction.
- When planning payroll and suppliers, I want a realistic collection forecast so that I can manage working capital.
- When a project closes, I want a complete commercial record so that retention and final-account value are not forgotten.
