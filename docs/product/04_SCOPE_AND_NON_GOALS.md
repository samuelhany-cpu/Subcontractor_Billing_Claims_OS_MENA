# Scope and Non-Goals

## MVP scope boundary

The MVP manages subcontractor-side commercial administration for one organization across multiple projects.

## Included

- Organization, users, roles, and project-level access
- Clients and projects
- Contract and amendment records
- BOQ baseline and import
- Progress valuation periods
- Payment applications and revisions
- Variation register and evidence
- Certification tracking
- Invoice and payment tracking
- Retention and advance recovery
- Cash forecast and aging
- Attachments, comments, notifications, and audit logs
- English and Arabic interfaces
- PDF and XLSX exports

## Explicit non-goals

### Accounting

The system does not provide:

- Chart of accounts
- Journal entries
- Trial balance
- Profit and loss statement
- Balance sheet
- Statutory tax filing

It may later integrate with accounting products.

### Project scheduling

The system does not replace Primavera P6, Microsoft Project, or planning tools. It stores period dates and may later import schedule references.

### Contract legal analysis

The system stores contractual terms and evidence. It does not determine legal entitlement or issue legal opinions.

### Procurement and supply chain

The MVP does not manage RFQs, purchase orders, warehouse stock, supplier invoices, or logistics.

### Full document management

The system stores commercial evidence and attachments but is not a full enterprise document management system.

### Main-contractor ERP

The initial product is optimized for subcontractors and specialist contractors. Main-contractor multi-tier subcontract administration is future scope.

## Change-control rule

Any requested feature outside MVP requires:

1. Problem statement
2. Named customer evidence
3. Revenue or adoption rationale
4. Architecture impact
5. Backlog priority decision
6. ADR when architecture changes
