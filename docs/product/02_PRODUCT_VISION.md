# Product Vision

## Working name

**Local Project Expense Tracker**

The name is descriptive and may be changed later.

## Vision statement

Replace the owner's project-expense Excel workbooks with a simple Arabic web application that runs on a Windows computer, is available on the private home/office network, and records project expenses and incoming transfers accurately.

## Core outcome

At any time, the owner can answer:

- How much money was received for a project?
- How much has been spent?
- What is the remaining available balance?
- How much was spent on materials versus works?
- How much was paid to a specific supplier or craftsman?
- How much remains against a payee agreement?
- What was spent today, this week, this month, or during a chosen period?
- Can the data be exported to a clean Excel file?
- Can a transaction be entered through Telegram and confirmed before saving?

## Product principles

1. Match the owner's real workflow.
2. Keep data entry faster than Excel.
3. Never infer missing financial facts.
4. Preserve a recoverable history.
5. No accounting jargon unless needed.
6. No taxes, branches, roles, or ERP modules.
7. Keep the interface Arabic, RTL, and light.
8. Keep the application usable by one person without a login screen.
9. Keep Telegram automation outside the core app in n8n.
10. Keep the app as the source of truth; n8n must use the API, not direct database writes.
